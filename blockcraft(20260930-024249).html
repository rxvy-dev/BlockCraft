World files use a compact binary .bcw format with an integrity checksum. WASD move · Space jump · Shift sprint · Mouse look · Left click mine/attack (hold to mine) · Right click place/eat/open table · F eat · E inventory + 2x2 crafting · 1-9 or wheel: hotbar
Click Play to lock the mouse; Esc pauses and unlocks it. Arrow keys also turn the camera. Right-click village doors to open or close them; right-click a bed to sleep. Punch logs, craft planks, a crafting table, then tools. Diamonds generate in deep underground stone around Y 4–16; mine Diamond Ore with a Stone Pickaxe or better. Caves can expose ores. Mobs drop items. Villages contain villagers, beds, crafting houses and loot chests. Night brings zombies, Endermen and rare Blazes. Endermen drop pearls; Blazes drop rods. Right-click villagers to trade (sell goods for Emeralds; the Librarian sells Eyes of Ender). Throw an Eye of Ender to point toward the buried stronghold, dig down to its lit End Portal, climb the obsidian towers to smash every End Crystal, then defeat the dragon and step into the exit portal.
